The 27th Annual Meeting of the Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue (SIGDIAL) 2026 will be held in **Atlanta, Georgia, August 2–5, 2026**. SIGDIAL brings together researchers and practitioners from both academia and industry to advance the frontiers of Conversational AI, Natural Language Processing, and Human-Computer Interaction.

We warmly invite you to join us at SIGDIAL 2026!

## Registration

Registration is now open. 
**Early registration** deadline is **July 6, 2026**, 11:59PM UTC-12:00 ("anywhere on Earth").
Please visit the [Registration](/registration/) page for the registration form and full details.

## Keynote Speakers

SIGDIAL 2026 features three distinguished keynote speakers:

* **August 3:** [Larry Heck](/speakers/larry_heck/) — Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Interactive Computing, Georgia Institute of Technology
* **August 4:** [Nanyun (Violet) Peng](/speakers/nanyun_peng/) — Associate Professor of Computer Science, University of California, Los Angeles
* **August 5:** [Dilek Hakkani-Tür](/speakers/dilek_hakkani_tur/) — Professor of Computer Science, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign

## Social Event

Join us for the conference social event on **August 4 (7:00 PM – 9:30 PM)** at [Sycamore Chapel](https://sycamorechapel.com). The event is included in the Main Conference + Workshops registration.

## Visa Support

If you require a visa to travel to the United States, we encourage you to begin the application process as early as possible. The registration form includes a field to request a visa support letter. We issue letters only to registrants who have completed registration and paid the applicable fee for their category.
