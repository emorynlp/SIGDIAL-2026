The 27th Annual Meeting of the Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue (SIGDIAL 2026) will be held in **Atlanta, Georgia, August 2–5, 2026**. SIGDIAL aims to bring together researchers and developers from both academia and industry worldwide.

We warmly invite your organization to support SIGDIAL 2026. Sponsorship is a meaningful opportunity to engage with a vibrant community advancing the frontiers of Conversational AI, Natural Language Processing, and Human-Computer Interaction.

## Sponsorship Tiers

We offer four levels of sponsorship, so you can choose the one that best fits your organization:

- **Platinum:** $2,500
- **Gold:** $1,500
- **Silver:** $1,000
- **Bronze:** $500

## Benefits


All sponsors receive the following benefits, regardless of tier:

- **Sponsor Booth** — A dedicated table during poster sessions and breaks, giving your organization direct visibility and meaningful face-to-face engagement with attendees.
- **One Free Registration** — One complimentary full conference registration for a representative of your organization.
