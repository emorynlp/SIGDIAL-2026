## Cancelation Policy

* Cancellations of registration up to one month prior to the conference (July 2) will be refunded the full amount paid (minus up to $50 processing fee).
* Cancellations up to three weeks prior to the conference (July 9) will be refunded any amounts paid over $200.
* Cancellations after three weeks prior to the conference (July 9) and all no-shows will not be refunded any amount.
