SIGDIAL'26 is rescheduled to August 2-5 to accommodate the [2026 FIFA World Cup](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/atlanta-host-eight-matches-usa-stadium) matches taking place between June 15 and July 15 in Altanta, GA.
